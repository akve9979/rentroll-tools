# Late fee policies

| Setting | Meaning | Birch Court default |
|---|---|---|
| `grace_days` | Days after the due date before a fee applies. 1 means the fee starts on the 2nd. | 1 |
| `flat_fee` | One-time fee on the first late day | $50.00 |
| `daily_fee` | Added for each further late day | $10.00 |
| `monthly_cap` | Most the fee can reach in one month. `None` means no cap. | $100.00 |

Check local rules before raising caps. Some states limit late fees to a share of the monthly rent.
